// Our Mission

No district left behind because of missing data

India has incredible infrastructure schemes — PMGSY, Jal Jeevan, BharatNet, PM-KUSUM. But they operate in silos. A road gets built with no power. Power reaches a village with no water. Nobody looks at all 4 layers together.

InfraLens was built to solve exactly that. We pull data from every government source and give decision-makers one unified view of where the real gaps are.

The Problem

Why Investment Fails

4 ministries, 4 dashboards, 0 integration. Nobody sees the full picture.

🔴 Siloed Data
4 ministries, 4 dashboards, 0 integration. Nobody sees the full infrastructure picture at district level.
🟡 Reactive Planning
Investments happen after failures, not before. No predictive risk system exists at district level.
🔵 No Prioritization
Budget goes to visible districts, not the most critical ones. Political bias without data-backed decisions.
🟢 Our Solution
InfraLens connects all 4 dots and ranks districts by true severity — not politics, not guesswork.
🤖 ML Prediction
6-month risk prediction lets planners act before a district becomes critical and expensive to fix.
📡 Scalable
Works for — districts today. Architecture built to scale to all 773 districts across India.

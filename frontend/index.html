Process

How InfraLens Works

From raw government data to actionable investment decisions in seconds.

01
Data Aggregation
Pull district-level data from data.gov.in, PMGSY, Jal Jeevan Mission, REC and TRAI — all 4 layers unified.
02
ML Scoring
Weighted model scores every district 0–100. Road 30%, Power 25%, Water 25%, Telecom 20%.
03
Risk Prediction
6-month deterioration risk using state factors, infrastructure imbalance and population density analysis.
04
Actionable Output
Prioritized interventions with scheme names, cost estimates and ROI projections — ready for budget planning.
